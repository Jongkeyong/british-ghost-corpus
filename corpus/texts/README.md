# Corpus Texts

This folder contains the 320 plain-text files included in version 0.4 of the British Ghost Corpus.

# Rights and Sources

The British Ghost Corpus contains texts first published between 1764 and 1913.

The source texts were collected from publicly accessible digital repositories,
including Project Gutenberg, Wikisource, and other online library collections.

The underlying literary works are believed to be in the public domain in the
United States. Digital transcriptions, translations, and repository-specific
materials remain subject to the terms identified in the corpus manifest.

Source URLs and access information are provided for research transparency.
No claim of copyright is made over the underlying public-domain literary texts.
